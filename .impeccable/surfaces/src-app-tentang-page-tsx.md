## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves Guyub's "gratis tapi ditanggung pribadi" claim by mirroring the transparency users already trust from fitur Kas, instead of a generic mission-statement "About Us" page: refuses the corporate About-page every SaaS ships.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits Guyub's committed system entirely, single self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans site-wide, warm primary (oranye kecoklatan) on cream background, `Card`/`StatCard`/`Badge` components, `rounded-[var(--radius)]`, the "Sebelum/Sesudah" and numbered-step patterns already in `src/app/page.tsx`. No new palette, type, or component language.

STORY: Visitor understands Guyub gratis karena Iman menanggung sendiri biaya infra, melihat angka nyata (jumlah tenant aktif, anggota aktif) sebagai bukti ini sistem yang benar-benar jalan bukan pitch kosong, melihat "struk" kategori biaya (server, domain, dst, tanpa nominal Rupiah) gaya baris Kas, lalu tergerak Trakteer sebagai bantuan personal ke pengembang. The Dream Company disebut sebagai studio asal Guyub dan tempat custom work lain dibuat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark Guyub + link kembali ke home) → hero stat row (2 StatCard: jumlah tenant aktif, anggota aktif) langsung di bawah headline pendek yang menyatakan premis "kenapa gratis", CTA Trakteer terlihat tanpa scroll di desktop.

FORM: "Rekap Transparan", posisi 1 dari 7 struktur grounded (dealt bareng posisi 7 dan lead posisi 3), seed key cf315824.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
